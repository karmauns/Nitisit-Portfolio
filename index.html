About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at Bangkok University, in the School of Information Technology and Innovation, with Data Science and Cyber Security as my major elective.

Most of my coursework has been on the security side, and it taught me more than I expected — how systems actually behave, how Linux and networks fit together, how much of security work is really just reading data carefully. But somewhere along the way I realised the part I kept enjoying wasn't the attack itself. It was everything underneath: where the data comes from, how it moves, and why pipelines break when volume goes up.

That's the direction I want to go — data engineering. I'm working on it with Python and SQL, cloud fundamentals through AWS and Oracle coursework, and the systems background my security studies gave me. Still early, but it's where I want to build.
