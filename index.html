About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at Bangkok University, in the School of Information Technology and Innovation, focusing on Data Science and Cyber Security as my major elective.

I like the space where those two overlap — data can tell you something's wrong, and security work is about figuring out why. I've picked up some of that through coursework and labs, mostly around penetration testing and a bit of detection work.

I also have some experience with web development, which just means I've seen things from both sides — building and breaking. That mix is what I find interesting.
